# Research harness reference study

Observed 2026-11-05 KST. This study informed hcodex's research workflow and its dedicated harness. It compared primary source code, contracts and evaluation implementations; it did not run an upstream benchmark or measure a quality/speed improvement for hcodex. The existing development DAG and Review Council retain their own semantics.

## Source observations and adoption decisions

OMH's [research skill](https://github.com/rlaope/oh-my-hermes/blob/dbe73279219b86cc9861d79c427d6d4db6b00b33/skills/ulw-research/SKILL.md) declares the question, boundaries, depth and uncertainty. Its [claims](https://github.com/rlaope/oh-my-hermes/blob/dbe73279219b86cc9861d79c427d6d4db6b00b33/src/workflows/research_claims.py), [progress](https://github.com/rlaope/oh-my-hermes/blob/dbe73279219b86cc9861d79c427d6d4db6b00b33/src/workflows/research_progress.py) and [dossier validator](https://github.com/rlaope/oh-my-hermes/blob/dbe73279219b86cc9861d79c427d6d4db6b00b33/src/workflows/research_dossier.py) enforce supplied structure and cross-links. Its [negative fixtures](https://github.com/rlaope/oh-my-hermes/blob/dbe73279219b86cc9861d79c427d6d4db6b00b33/tests/test_research_dossier.py) cover malformed links and partial outcomes. These checks do not authenticate a source, fetch a URL, prove a file was read, or establish quote entailment. hcodex therefore records host-observed retrieval receipts and keeps semantic truth separate from structural integrity.

Open Deep Research's [configuration](https://github.com/langchain-ai/open_deep_research/blob/1b7d2e80db9faa586165c60e09096dbbfd483a64/src/open_deep_research/configuration.py) and [supervisor implementation](https://github.com/langchain-ai/open_deep_research/blob/1b7d2e80db9faa586165c60e09096dbbfd483a64/src/open_deep_research/deep_researcher.py) bound investigation and loops. Its [evaluators](https://github.com/langchain-ai/open_deep_research/blob/1b7d2e80db9faa586165c60e09096dbbfd483a64/tests/evaluators.py) use model judgments; hcodex adopts the dimensions, while keeping those judgments outside mechanical CI.

STORM's [information/outline/article pipeline](https://github.com/stanford-oval/storm/blob/fb951af7744dab086e34962e9bc6fe878e145f83/knowledge_storm/storm_wiki/engine.py) and [section generation](https://github.com/stanford-oval/storm/blob/fb951af7744dab086e34962e9bc6fe878e145f83/knowledge_storm/storm_wiki/modules/article_generation.py) keep source references connected to report sections. hcodex makes the final body host-rendered from known claim IDs, preserving unresolved and refuted findings rather than accepting arbitrary synthesis prose as verified fact.

Anthropic describes [research orchestration](https://www.anthropic.com/research/multi-agent-research-system) and [evaluation layers](https://www.anthropic.com/research/demystifying-evals-for-ai-agents). [DeepResearch Bench](https://deepresearch-bench.github.io/) separates report quality from citation/retrieval quality. These motivate two hcodex evidence lanes: a deterministic fixed-corpus integrity harness in `make ci`, and separately recorded live capability/semantic-quality evaluations. An external score or several agreeing agents does not establish hcodex correctness.

## hcodex contract

- Existing code DAG build gates, workspace settlement and Triad votes retain their meaning.
- Research consumes bounded repository/code, local technical-document and public HTTPS sources. A question-only request defaults to one bounded native Codex discovery turn; explicit-source requests search only when `--web` is selected, and `--no-web` retains the source-required plan behavior. PDF parsing, crawling and historical-source proof remain outside this scope.
- Native discovery is grounded in Codex CLI's official [JSONL output](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/non-interactive-mode#make-output-machine-readable) and [web-search security](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/agent-approvals-security#traffic-outside-the-command-network-proxy) contracts. hcodex counts exact completed `web_search` item IDs. Its event cap bounds observable tool events, not provider-internal query expansion.
- Discovery emits bounded credential-free HTTPS candidates only. Each candidate must normalize to a `results[].url` from a completed observed `web_search` item; free-form model output cannot introduce one. hcodex does not copy raw queries, snippets or reasoning into its database/report or use them as citations. Native Codex sessions may retain their own provider evidence under provider policy. The host separately retrieves each accepted URL and assigns source/excerpt IDs with locator, time, full-body digest and bounded excerpt/location. A candidate remains unobserved until this succeeds.
- Discovery requires confirmed actual-model identity and owned process-group closure. Its stable ID is bound to the parent and effective versioned configuration; same-ID reads do not rediscover or retry unknown work.
- Failed discovered candidates are retained as optional rejections, while failed explicit sources remain required failures. Investigation begins only after the configured accepted-source minimum is met.
- Investigation, counter-evidence review and synthesis use existing model routes and owned jobs; the parent alone writes the aggregate.
- Final sections refer to known claim IDs. The host renders their text/citations and keeps contested, unresolved and refuted results visible.
- Runtime and harness corpus use the same Rust validator. Its proof concerns schema, bounds, source/excerpt linkage and quote fidelity. Semantic support and truth remain `NOT_MECHANICALLY_PROVEN`.
- Cancellation, unknown outcomes, missing evidence and exhausted budgets remain explicit partial results under their original request IDs. A research result is never build/test proof.

The project-owned runtime policy is `crates/hcodex/assets/research-rules.md`, and the regression corpus is `crates/hcodex/tests/fixtures/research_contract_cases.json`. `crates/hcodex/research-assets.toml` pins their hashes and deterministic verification contract. Local `.agents/` materializations are ignored development inputs and are not required by public builds. The project adaptation is not represented as an unchanged upstream or Alexandria materialization.
